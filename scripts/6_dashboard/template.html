How this action works
Assumptions and glossary
Healthcare stigma
Share of men who were afraid of, or avoided, healthcare because someone might learn they have sex with men (AMIS). Adjusted for the 2022 change in the question.
PrEP
Pre-exposure prophylaxis: medicine that prevents HIV. Measured as use in the past year.
PrEP gap
The difference between current PrEP use and the share of men who use it or are willing to.
Feedback
Whether growing PrEP use itself changes stigma: lowering it (PrEP becomes normal) or raising it (judgement of PrEP users). Fitted to the 2018–2024 trends, a modest raising effect fits best (+0.15, plausible range about −0.07 to +0.29); the dashboard shows that value, no effect, and a lowering effect for affirming care.
Model runs
The model is run many times with different plausible inputs; ranges show where 95% of runs fall.
